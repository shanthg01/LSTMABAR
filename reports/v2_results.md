## Discussion

**P6 exit gate: not met (documented negative result for stage 1).** The gate asks for a model that beats the baselines on `test_gold` with non-overlapping 95 % CIs; without real human items it is judged on each LLM-written gold set separately (D9), and claims are "on LLM-written test instructions".

- The main stage-1 model (`p6_model`: frozen CLAP text + audio embeddings, physics mapping, 5 seeds) is not better than the best baseline, kNN-bge, on any gold set, and is worse on `llm_draft`: paired 2AFC difference −0.107 [−0.179, −0.039] (Wilcoxon p = 0.005).
- The best variant, `p6_bge_text` (bge-base text embedding instead of CLAP text; 5 seeds), ties kNN-bge: paired differences −0.024 [−0.074, +0.030], +0.001 [−0.059, +0.059] and −0.012 [−0.077, +0.054] on simulated_human, llm_persona and llm_draft. Its gold CIs overlap kNN-bge's and mean params' on every set.

**In distribution the model does help.** On the synthetic `test` split (performer-disjoint DI, template + paraphrase captions like training), `p6_bge_text` has lower MR-STFT than kNN-bge (1.133 vs 1.263; paired −0.130 [−0.173, −0.085]), lower masked param L1 (0.112 vs 0.121, CIs overlap) and higher gate accuracy (0.960 vs 0.943, CIs overlap). That gain does not transfer to the held-out gold instructions, so it reflects fitting the caption distribution better, not better text understanding.

**Unseen pedal (RAT holdout).** No method is clearly better than mean params on `test_unseen_pedal` MR-STFT (mean 1.850; kNN-CLAP 1.825 is nominally lower, CIs overlap; `p6_bge_text` 1.882, paired vs kNN-bge −0.021 [−0.128, +0.082]); `p6_bge_text` also has lower gate accuracy there (0.939 vs 1.000 for mean). Retrieval and the learned predictor both fail to generalize to the unseen circuit.

**Ablations (val, 5 seeds each; the selection split, not a test result).**
- The text encoder is the dominant factor: bge text reaches masked param L1 0.0963 ± 0.0004 and gate accuracy 0.964, vs 0.1151 ± 0.0005 and 0.936 for CLAP text (main). The other CLAP checkpoint (`larger_clap_general`) is slightly worse than the main one (0.1167).
- The CLAP audio input adds nothing: text only (0.1133) and shuffled audio (0.1134) match or beat the main model (0.1151); audio only is far worse (0.1701, and it never turns the compressor on: compressor gate F1 0). The model does not use the input clip to adapt the settings, which the design assumed it would (design §4.1).
- The physics mapping and the unconstrained mapping are indistinguishable (0.1151 vs 0.1149).

**Text2FX (Colab, first 100 items per split; gold column = `llm_draft` only).** On the same items, Text2FX's gold 2AFC is 0.601 [0.536, 0.664] (69 headline items): above chance and close to mean params (0.616), below kNN-bge (0.710). On synthetic `test` it is the worst method on MR-STFT (2.944, worse than no-op's 2.500) and masked param L1 (0.373). CLAP similarity rises from 0.154 to 0.345 for the returned boards, so the optimization works on its own objective; the objective is a poor proxy for the target tone, as spike A predicted (CLAP tracks the drive-amount contrast only; see the per-axis table in [reports/text2fx_subset100.md](text2fx_subset100.md) (generated by `eval run --limit 100`, hence the "smoke" wording inside it; its "dirty tree" stamp comes from uncommitted generated report files in the local checkout that wrote it), where its gain-axis 2AFC is the highest of its axes).

**Not done / caveats.**
- LLM zero-shot is not in these tables (needs the owner's API key); stage 2 (`L_audio` through the pedalboard, Colab) and the listening test (6.6) are not done. Stage 2 is the remaining lever for the gold result.
- All gold sets are LLM-written; the training paraphrases are also Claude-written, so these sets are closer in style to the training captions than real people's would be (D9).
- Model selection used val only (early stopping on the parameter part of the D8 proxy); gold was never used for selection. Wilcoxon p-values are uncorrected for multiple comparisons.
- Seed variance is small throughout (seed SD of the gold 2AFC means ≤ 0.02), so the conclusions are not seed noise.
