# White-box calibration tables (P4 R4)

Per white-box pedal, the grey-box (`Drive` + `EQ3`) label fitted to the circuit simulation on a gain × tone knob grid (`data/calibration/<pedal>.json`), against the derived (unfitted) label at the same settings. Metric: the fidelity gate's masked mean harmonic error in dB (`physics.fidelity`, P2 decision 6), on the calibration inputs below. Fits are selected in-sample (an expressiveness bound on these inputs, as in the fidelity check).

Generated by `lstmabar data calibrate`; runtime 5476 s on CPU (10 torch threads); git 312be33d34857aedfec5e7acb6fce7a4e6c64c22 (dirty). Per-pedal summaries (jumps, fallback cells, derived-label jumps) recomputed from the written tables by `lstmabar data calibrate --report-only` (no refit).

## Procedure

- Inputs: sines at 82.41, 146.83, 329.63 Hz × -24, -15, -6 dBFS peak at 44100 Hz (white-box 4x oversampled, grey-box Drive 4x), 0.15 s settle + 0.5 s analysed (fit on the first 0.2 s).
- Grid: 5 gain × 3 tone rotations evenly spaced in [0, 1]; level knob at its pot default during fits (applied analytically at lookup from the derivation's post-clip gain).
- Fit: the 11 Drive + EQ3 knobs, 200 Adam steps per grid point (`physics.fidelity.fit`, lr 0.05 cosine), loss = differentiable harmonic metric only (the fidelity check's riff MR-STFT term is off: on a 1 s riff at 44.1 kHz it cost ~10x the sines per step). Gain index 0: derived start + 1 random start(s), best kept; every next gain point is warm-started from the previous gain point's fit at the same tone. Unfitted knobs come from the derived preset; compressor off.
- Lookup: bilinear interpolation of normalized params over the gain × tone cell; if any two of the cell's corners differ by more than max_jump = 0.25 (max abs normalized difference over knobs), the nearest grid point is used instead (`calibrated_nearest`). Gates are copied.
